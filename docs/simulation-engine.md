# Safe Simulation Engine

The engine validates a scenario and target, snapshots the modeled state, evaluates controls, propagates only through synthetic same-facility dependencies, derives business impact, and records simulation and incident events.

`MFA` stops account compromise at the identity boundary. `LEAST_PRIVILEGE` blocks modeled data-system access. `NETWORK_SEGMENTATION` blocks IT-to-OT asset types. The What-If endpoint performs the same deterministic projection with hardened controls without modifying the primary operational twin.
